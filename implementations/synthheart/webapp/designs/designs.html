SynthHeart · three design directions

Same model, same data and same features; only the layout, style and motion differ. Pick one to take forward.

Design AClinical Console

EHR-style workspace: sidebar navigation, grouped clinical sections, dense and calm. Best for a desk or workstation.
Design BCare Dashboard

Modern care-team dashboard: gradient header, sliders, ring gauge, soft cards. Best for demos and laptops.
Design CBedside Assessment

Tablet-first, step-by-step wizard with large touch targets and a full-screen result. Best at the bedside.
