Method

How the estimate is made

From real patient records to a risk model that never saw a real patient.

What this estimate is

  • A gradient-boosted model (XGBoost) trained on synthetic patients produced by a diffusion model (TabDDPM).
  • On real patients it never saw, it ranks risk almost as well as a model trained on real data: AUC vs .
  • It reflects a public cardiovascular dataset (70,000 adults, roughly 30–65 years), not a clinical population.
  • “What moved this estimate” shows how each input shifted the model's score from the average. It explains the model, not the patient's biology.

Limits

  • Not a diagnostic tool. Use clinical judgement and validated risk scores for care decisions.
  • Synthetic patients cluster around typical profiles (coverage 0.50), so unusual patients are estimated less reliably.
  • The source data has entry errors and coarse values; impossible rows were removed with medical-range rules.
  • Accuracy is about : roughly one in four estimates lands on the wrong side of 50%.
